## 🎯 What is Breakout?

Breakout is a legendary arcade game that revolutionized the gaming industry. Players control a paddle to bounce a ball and break bricks, combining simple mechanics with challenging gameplay that has captivated players since 1976.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Move the paddle left and right
2. Bounce the ball to break bricks
3. Don't let the ball fall below the paddle
4. Clear all bricks to advance
5. Collect power-ups for special abilities

## 🌟 Historical Significance

Originally developed by Atari in 1976, Breakout represents a pivotal moment in gaming history. Created by Nolan Bushnell and Steve Bristow, with contributions from Steve Jobs and Steve Wozniak, the game has influenced countless titles and remains a benchmark in arcade gaming.
